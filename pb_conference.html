PitchBook Conference

I led the design of the PitchBook conference app, a cross-platform application, used by top organizations in the industry.

About the Project

PitchBook partners with top industry organizations around the world and provides custom mobile apps and web platforms for their conferences, giving attendees special access to PitchBook's database as well as a suite of conference features.

Challenges

Showcase Data

PitchBook partners with more than 40 conferences and gives access to more than 5000 people a year.

The Conference app is a channel for PitchBook to showcase the breadth of the data and expose the platform to more potential clients.

Cross-platform

Since the app could be used on both web and mobile, we worked hard to ensure a coherent experience.

We identified key work-flows for each platform and took a mobile first approach.

Constraints

One of our biggest challenges is to design a framework that would work with different types of conferences.

We studied multiple use cases across many conferences and to found commonalities to design for.

Wireframes & Iterations

A few wireframes for the conference App

Attendee Profiles

The Conference app is built on top of the regular PitchBook app, so we needed to find a way to differentiate between a regular PitchBook profile and a conference profile for attendees.

Conference Home

We wanted a home page that can provide value right when users log in, so we explored different types of information to show on the home page.

In-App Messaging

Much of the success in the PE & VC industry comes from leveraging people's personal networks. Attending conferences is an opportunity for professionals to meet people and extend their personal networks.

The messaging feature is designed to facilitate this interaction, allowing users to message any attendee or speaker directly within the app.

Scheduling

We found out that majority of the users log into the web platform days prior to the conference to set up their schedules. They send out many meeting invitations beforehand and use the mobile app as a reference when they arrive at the conference.

As a result, we optimized the web version for the scheduling the work-flow, such as proposing a new time, and optimized the mobile version for more browsing related tasks.

Agenda

Like the scheduling feature, many users set up their schedule before the conference and use the mobile app as a reference when they arrive at the conference.

To help improve this work-flow, we added search and filters in the web version, so they can easily filter through the events on the agenda to add them to their schedule.

Result

The conference app that is used by more than 40 conferences a year. It generates ~10% of our total leads and is also one of our main channels of marketing.
